# How It Works

Power, with backup built in. Here's how it works.
Base Power is more than just affordable power. A backup battery at your home protects your family from blackouts — and helps steady the grid everyone depends on.

## Running on grid
Base works with the utility you already have.
In some areas Base becomes your energy provider. In others — like co-op and municipal territories — your utility stays exactly as it is, and Base simply adds a battery to your home.
Which one applies to you depends on who delivers your power.
Same home. Same wiring. The power in your walls doesn't change.

## The battery
The battery is what makes Base, Base.
It gives you seamless, automatic backup during outages and helps stabilize the grid when there's no outage.
Switches over in under 1 second. Whole-home protection.

Your battery shifts with the grid. It charges when energy is cheap — like overnight — and may share some power back when the grid is stressed. That's how we help stabilize the system.
So if your charge level isn't always full, that's expected. We always keep backup in reserve. Your battery charge will fluctuate, but it rarely drops to the minimum we hold back for you.
We monitor weather and forecast outages so we can protect your backup — keeping you powered for as long as possible.

## Outage behavior
When the grid goes down, your battery takes over. Your backup kicks in, usually under half a second.
A single battery can back up your home up to 36 hours. Exact runtime depends on your power usage.
Your home must be using <20 kW for the battery to turn on. If high-draw devices push usage higher, you can use the Base App to turn on the battery after reducing usage.

## Sign-up process
Step 1 — See if Base is a fit for your home.
You can talk with one of our energy advisors, or take your time exploring. Enter your zip code to check your home's eligibility and next steps.

Step 2 — We'll review your setup.
"Our engineers will confirm whether your home qualifies — and if so, for how many batteries (0, 1, or 2)." From there, we'll handle all the paperwork and logistics.

Step 3 — Installation. We've got it covered.
Your install happens in two visits. On the first, we install the electrical equipment and get your home ready for the battery.
Within three weeks we're back to install the battery outside near your meter. No one needs to be home, and your power stays on.
After that, your home is backed up, connected, and ready for whatever comes next.

## Flexibility and install details
We coordinate everything with your utility to make sure you're all squared away. Where Base becomes your energy provider, we handle the switch from your current provider too.

Built to be flexible, even if you move: "The battery agreement runs with the home. If the new homeowner signs up for Base, everything transfers easily. If not, we'll remove the system — no matter when you move."

Installed for your home, not just any home: "We find a clean, code-compliant spot for your system close to your electric meter, and install a soft start if needed to support larger HVAC systems. Every install is tailored with care, based on what works best for your home and the National Electric Code."

## Certifications
Base Power Development, LLC battery systems are certified under standards UL 1973, UL 1741, UL 9540, and UL 9540A for safety and performance.
