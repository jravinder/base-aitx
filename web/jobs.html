Rule 02Simulation

Work moves on the feeder

0GPU-hours lost

With two or more nodes on a feeder, 120 nodes over 3 days.

  • sync_altWhen a node stops, a neighbor on the same feeder takes its job next and keeps the progress.
  • home_pinJobs prefer their own node, then the feeder. Moving data costs $0 on the node, $0.10 on the feeder, $0.40 farther.

Rule 03Simulation

Price-driven pause

$400/MWh

Grid price where the lowest-rate batch job pauses: $0.16 per GPU-hour over a 0.4 kW GPU.

  • trending_upA GPU runs a job while the job's rate beats its power cost. Higher tiers keep running to higher prices; member jobs to $3,000/MWh.
  • battery_charging_fullThe battery sells in the four highest-price hours of the day ahead and charges in the lowest.
