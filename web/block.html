Why a station

Base told us on the factory tour that in a Dallas-area storm, one street with test batteries kept its power, and neighbours walked over.

A planning hypothesis for Base to confirm: library participation, site capacity and outage operation.

Library station: 4 batteries and 4 PCs. One home: 1 battery and 1 PC. Real ERCOT day-ahead prices; PCs busy 40% of the time is a planning dial. From grid/LIBRARY_NODE.md and grid/NUMBERS.md. Member nodes from data/members.json and web/data/tower_replay.json.

Sources: Austin Public Library Locations (tc36-hn4j), via house/public_buildings.json, and City of Austin issued permits via data/mirror/energy_permits.csv.gz, as of the capture date. Permit locations are rounded to 4 decimals and show filing activity. Locations only, no street addresses. Check current public records when assessing a site.
