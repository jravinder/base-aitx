1 · Three offers

Use, Host, Sell

Our proposal

Same shape as the home battery: hardware sits at or near the house, the member is served first, and spare capacity serves others.

OfferWho paysWho owns the hardwareWhere it runsWhat the member gets
UseMember picks an open model and asks privately. The member, per GPU-hour. Member tier $1.20 Simulation The member's home PC, or Base's station on the feeder. Home PC, or the station on the member's feeder. Private answers from the model they choose. Pay for GPU time, with no per-token fee to a frontier provider.
HostMember keeps a GPU at home, or Base installs one next to the house. Buyers of the spare GPU time: businesses, Base, partners, marketplace. The member, or Base when Base installs it. Like a battery at the house. At the member's house, on battery-backed power. A share of the revenue from time sold. The sim gives the gross per home; the member's share is a dial to set.
SellBase sells spare station time to local businesses. Small and medium businesses and individuals. Business tier $1.09 Simulation Base station. In an isolated sandbox on the station. Member questions run first and the backup reserve holds. Station revenue pays for the local compute members use.
